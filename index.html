01 / Prospecting

Find the right people to talk to

For a loyalty and rewards agency, I find the right contacts at target companies, usually national trade marketing managers and commercial heads, and keep the list tight to general trade companies, not e-commerce. I've also built B2B prospect lists from Inc42 and Tracxn for email campaigns.

02 / Outreach

High-volume calls and emails

I've cold-called car dealerships in Australia for an AI voice product, then moved to calling prospects in India and Southeast Asia for the loyalty agency. Different markets, different time zones, same job: get someone on the line and earn the next minute.

03 / Qualification

Understand the need before booking the meeting

When prospects request a meeting, I call them first to qualify before the intro: do they already run a programme, am I speaking to the decision maker, how many retailers and distributors they work with, and whether there's budget. You can try that checklist further down the page.

04 / Meetings

Book discovery calls for the senior team

I booked 5 client meetings for the India market in September 2026, handing qualified prospects over for the intro call.

05 / Records

Keep activity and lead status accurate

Outbound only works with follow-ups, and follow-ups only work if the notes are right. I'm usually the one with the tracker open before the call ends. At TING Works I managed workflows across Zoho Projects, Zoho Cliq and Meta Business Suite.

06 / Resilience

Handle rejection and keep going

On my Australia calls I heard "not interested", "send me an email" and "call me later" more than anything else. When calls weren't getting picked up, I switched to early-morning calling and more people answered. Turning pickups into booked meetings is the skill I'm building right now, and a role with real training and mentorship is where I want to build it.

07 / Customer-facing

Build rapport and communicate clearly

A month of customer support and member care on Capillary taught me how much tone matters when someone is frustrated. On the writing side, I planned content for 10+ brands at TING Works and work on Greentern's LinkedIn and client newsletter, so my emails sound like a person wrote them.

08 / Targets

Meet and beat the monthly number

At Greentern, success is measured by how many customers I actually get to speak to, and I report my meetings to my manager every month. I like knowing exactly what the number is and where I stand against it.
